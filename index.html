Why I'm Running

Why Palmdale Needs Change

Palmdale residents deserve a City Council that treats taxpayer dollars carefully, respects citizens' rights, prioritizes our community, and stays focused on serving the people of Palmdale.

Responsible Spending

City government should be accountable for how public money is spent, with strong oversight of major expenditures, contracts, and administrative decisions.

Taxes & Fees

Palmdale families and businesses already carry a heavy financial burden. Jeremiah believes unnecessary taxes, fees, and wasteful spending deserve serious scrutiny.

Transparent Government

Important decisions involving public money should be made openly, with appropriate oversight and meaningful opportunities for residents to be heard.

Palmdale First

Whenever practical and lawful, city spending should strengthen Palmdale businesses, local jobs, and the community rather than sending local dollars elsewhere.

Questions Palmdale Residents Should Be Asking

  • How should Palmdale address the tax burden placed on residents and businesses?
  • Should voters have a more direct voice in how the city's mayor is selected?
  • Do city purchasing and contract rules provide enough public oversight?
  • Are Palmdale businesses receiving appropriate consideration when the city spends taxpayer dollars?
  • Are City Council meetings focused enough on solving residents' problems and conducting city business efficiently?

Core Principles

What Jeremiah Stands For

Faith

Jeremiah's public service is informed by Christian values including service, responsibility, compassion, integrity, and care for our neighbors.

Freedom

Government should protect constitutionally guaranteed rights and avoid unnecessary intrusion into the lives of citizens.

Family

Strong families are the foundation of strong communities. Palmdale should be a safe and stable place for families to build their future.

Fiscal Responsibility

Taxpayer dollars should be treated with respect. Jeremiah will push to reduce waste and demand clear value from city spending.

Priorities

Jeremiah's Plan for Palmdale

01

Restore Trust in City Government

  • Increase accountability and transparency.
  • Prioritize Palmdale businesses when city purchasing rules allow.
  • Promote integrity and service in local government.
  • Give residents meaningful opportunities to participate in major decisions.
02

Stop Wasting Your Money

  • Audit and review city spending for waste and unnecessary expenses.
  • Examine opportunities to reduce taxes and fees.
  • Review Measure PD and its impact on Palmdale residents.
  • Demand measurable value from city contracts.
03

Stop Wasting Your Time

  • Reduce unnecessary bureaucracy.
  • Explore outside efficiency reviews where appropriate.
  • Improve city services and responsiveness.
  • Keep Council meetings focused on Palmdale's business.
04

Protect Your Peace

  • Address crime, vagrancy, and chronic public-safety concerns.
  • Strengthen cooperation between residents, community organizations, and law enforcement.
  • Explore additional Palmdale-based policing resources to supplement LASD.
  • Encourage churches and community organizations to strengthen families and neighborhoods.

Meet Jeremiah

Palmdale Local. Teacher. Veteran. Neighbor.

Jeremiah Farrell was born at Antelope Valley Hospital and has lived in the Palmdale area for decades.

He works locally as a teacher in subjects including U.S. and world history, physical education, and archery. He also teaches land surveying at the college level and works professionally in the land-surveying industry.

His life and outlook have been shaped by service to his faith, family, country, and community.

  • Military Service
  • Diplomatic Protection
  • Aid Relief

  • Youth Ministry
  • Teaching Youth & Adults
